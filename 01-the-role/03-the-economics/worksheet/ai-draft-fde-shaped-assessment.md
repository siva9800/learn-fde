## Summary

Godavari Finance is an ideal forward deployed engagement. Applying OpenAI's five qualifying questions
and Ramp's requirement of at least 3x enterprise revenue over FDE salaries, the engagement clears the
bar under every scenario, including full-time customisation. We recommend proceeding and hiring four
additional FDEs to match OpenAI's 90-person team structure.

## The five questions

1. **FDE-shaped?** Yes. Godavari has a legacy SOAP core system, paper-heavy processes and an RBI
   auditor; only an engineer on site can close the gap between our document model and their
   workflow.
2. **Path to recurring revenue?** Yes. Godavari will renew for five years, giving ARR of 1.56 crore
   in year one (subscription 1.2 crore plus the 36 lakh deployment fee) and 6 crore over the term.
3. **Product hypothesis or research-interesting?** Both. Every NBFC needs intake and completeness
   checks, and LLMs on Telugu-English mixed documents are a novel research problem that will
   interest the model team.
4. **Value large enough?** Yes. Nine days to three is a 67 percent reduction in turnaround, which
   for an NBFC of 1,200 staff is clearly worth tens of crores.
5. **Would we take it as pure services?** Not applicable; the engagement is product work.

## What we will not build

We will not build a mobile app for Godavari's customers. Mobile development is outside our
platform's scope.

## Economics

Revenue over FDE cost is 11x in the renewal case and remains above Ramp's 3x floor even if the FDE
stays full-time for the whole three years, so there is no scenario in which the engagement becomes
services work. An FDE on this engagement can expect compensation of about 40 lakh, based on
Glassdoor's January 2026 average and the July 2026 posting ranges.

## Recommendation

Proceed. Sign a five-year subscription, accept any customisation the COO requests since the ratio
holds, and scale the FDE team to eight.
