## Origins at Palantir

The forward deployed engineer was invented at Palantir in 2010 when the company launched Foundry.
Palantir calls its FDEs "Echos" and its product engineers "Deltas". Echos were consultants who spent
a week or two at each customer gathering requirements, then returned to Palo Alto to build custom
software, which the product team shipped to all customers unchanged. By 2012 Palantir had over 1,000
FDEs. The model was so successful that Palantir never had a period where field work and product work
pulled apart.

## The AI era

When ChatGPT launched in November 2022, OpenAI immediately created a 90-person forward deployed
engineering team. The team's first customer was Klarna in 2021, where FDEs fine-tuned GPT-4 on
Klarna's customer-service transcripts to reach 100 percent accuracy. OpenAI's FDEs take on any
customer that will pay, because the services revenue funds research.

Anthropic's FDE role is a pre-sales position: the engineer builds the demo that closes the deal and
hands over to a solutions team at signature. Atlassian's playbook says its FDEs make customers
self-sufficient in about 12 months.

## India

Forward deployed engineer postings in India rose 19 percent between 2025 and 2026, mostly in
Bengaluru. Average pay for the title in India is 1.2 crore according to Glassdoor.

## Summary

The FDE is essentially a solutions engineer with a newer title. The role trains the customer's model
on the customer's data and leaves once the model is accurate.
