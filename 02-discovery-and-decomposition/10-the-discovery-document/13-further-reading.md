## Writing documents that get decisions

| Resource | Why read it |
|---|---|
| Amazon, *2017 Letter to Shareholders* (published April 2018): https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/2017-letter-to-shareholders (fetched October 2026) | "narratively structured six-page memos" read silently at the start of meetings; why great memos take a week, not a day; the idea behind the pre-read |
| Malte Ubl, *Design Docs at Google* (6 July 2020): https://www.industrialempathy.com/posts/design-docs-at-google/ (fetched October 2026) | context and scope that link rather than repeat; non-goals as "things that could reasonably be goals, but are explicitly chosen not to be goals", which is the will-not-do list in engineering form |
| Michael Nygard, *Documenting Architecture Decisions* (15 November 2011): https://cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions (fetched October 2026) | the architecture decision record: context, decision, status, consequences; superseded rather than deleted; the model for the decision log |
| Atlassian Team Playbook, *DACI decision-making framework*: https://www.atlassian.com/team-playbook/plays/daci (fetched October 2026) | Driver, Approver, Contributors, Informed; one approver per decision; record the final decision in the shared document |
