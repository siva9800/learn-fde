## Discovery and interviewing

| Resource | Why read it |
|---|---|
| getperspective.ai, *The FDE discovery playbook: validating requirements before you build* (9 July 2026): https://getperspective.ai/blog/fde-discovery-playbook-validating-requirements-before-you-build-2026 | fetched: five phases (scope, interview, synthesise, validate, spec); interview at least 8 to 10 end users, not just the sponsor; ask about the last failure; synthesise every three or four interviews; a requirement needs three independent interviews; "name the specific human whose day gets better"; eval co-design by week three. The vendor sells interview tooling |
| getperspective.ai, *How forward deployed engineers run customer discovery in 2026* (14 May 2026): https://getperspective.ai/blog/how-forward-deployed-engineers-run-customer-discovery-2026 | fetched: "interview at least 10 end users before writing production code"; argues for 15 to 40 async interviews; the five Week 0 roles including the sceptic ("the highest-leverage hour of Week 0") and the eval owner; "walk me through the last time you did X"; "the weird Tuesday" cases; "variance matters more than volume" |
| Atlassian, *Forward Deployed Engineering Playbook* (4 October 2026 on fetch): https://www.atlassian.com/blog/guides-research/forward-deployed-engineering-playbook | fetched: map "who is involved, which systems hold the necessary knowledge, where handoffs break down"; building alongside teams in their environment; frequent, painful, measurable. The summary page does not discuss shadowing; the linked PDF has the full playbook (not fetched) |
| cloudsoftsol, *FDE first 90 days*: https://cloudsoftsol.com/blog/fde-first-90-days | cited by the course plan for "days 1 to 2 access and reality" and the golden set before model choice. On fetch the page was a 30-60-90 plan dated 7 October 2026 without that phrase; it puts discovery in days 31 to 60 and recommends "a small fixed test set of real questions with expected answers, agreed with a customer subject-matter expert", with no count. The 100 to 200 figure is the course's (Part 2, Day 9) |
| OpenAI forward deployed engineering, ZenML LLMOps database summaries: https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/forward-deployed-engineering-bringing-enterprise-llm-applications-to-production and https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/forward-deployed-engineering-for-enterprise-llm-adoption | experts involved before building; evals as the contract (not re-fetched for this chapter) |
| conikeec, *The FDE playbook: a practitioner's field manual*: https://conikeec.substack.com/p/the-forward-deployed-engineer-playbook | a practitioner's account of embedding with the customer (not re-fetched) |
| Rob Fitzpatrick, *The Mom Test* (2013) | the best short book on asking about past behaviour instead of opinions; written for founders, and every rule applies to operators |
| Steve Portigal, *Interviewing Users* (Rosenfeld Media, second edition 2023) | note-taking, silence, follow-ups, synthesis; a researcher's craft book |
| Hugh Beyer and Karen Holtzblatt, *Contextual Design* (1998) | the original method for watching work in place: the master and apprentice model behind shadowing |
