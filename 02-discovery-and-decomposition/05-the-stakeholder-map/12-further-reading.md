## The FDE playbooks on stakeholders

| Resource | Why read it |
|---|---|
| getperspective.ai, *How forward deployed engineers run customer discovery in 2026*: https://getperspective.ai/blog/how-forward-deployed-engineers-run-customer-discovery-2026 | the week-0 stakeholder map; the five personas (executive sponsor, end users, eval owner, admin and IT, sceptic) with questions to ask and avoid; "the highest-leverage hour of Week 0"; the eval owner as "not the sponsor"; interview at least ten end users before production code (Day 6 owns the interviews) |
| getperspective.ai, *The FDE discovery playbook: validating requirements before you build*: https://getperspective.ai/blog/fde-discovery-playbook-validating-requirements-before-you-build-2026 | "the specific human whose day gets better"; the sponsor's requirements as "a well-formatted guess" until operators confirm them |
| Atlassian, *Forward Deployed Engineering Playbook*: https://www.atlassian.com/blog/guides-research/forward-deployed-engineering-playbook | "map who is involved, which systems hold the necessary knowledge, where handoffs break down"; "build permissions, security and auditability in from day one"; "curiosity drives initial adoption, value drives sustained usage" (Day 33 owns adoption) |
| OpenAI forward deployed engineering, summarised in the ZenML LLMOps database: https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/forward-deployed-engineering-bringing-enterprise-llm-applications-to-production and https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/forward-deployed-engineering-for-enterprise-llm-adoption | the customer's experts involved before building; evals as the contract between vendor and customer, which is the eval-owner split in practice (Day 14 builds it) |
| cloudsoftsol, *FDE first 90 days*: https://cloudsoftsol.com/blog/fde-first-90-days | days 1 to 2 as access and reality; the security conversation early; the shape Day 8 dates |
| conikeec, *The forward deployed engineer playbook: a practitioner's field manual*: https://conikeec.substack.com/p/the-forward-deployed-engineer-playbook | a practitioner's account of sponsors, champions and the adoption problem across engagements |
