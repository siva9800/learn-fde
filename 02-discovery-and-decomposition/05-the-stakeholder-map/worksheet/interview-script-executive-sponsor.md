# Mapping conversation · Executive sponsor

> Day 5 · thirty minutes, usually less · run in week 0, after the Day 4 problem has been chosen
> Purpose: fill the sponsor's row (the number, the promise upward, the calendar) and find the rows the sponsor knows about and did not mention.
> At Godavari: the COO.

**Before you sit down:** have the Day 4 problem statement in front of you, and the seven candidates he brought, so you can refer to the one he chose. Do not bring the demo deck.

## The number (minutes 0 to 5)

| Ask | Listen for | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| "When this works, what will you say to the board, in one sentence?" | "loan turnaround is three days"; the date it has to be true | the number they would believe; what was promised upward |
| "What is on the slide today, and what number does it come from?" | nine days from the core system's two dates; nothing about waiting | their version, and the data gap behind it |
| "By when?" | the year-end board, the RBI cycle | the calendar; Day 9's target date |

## The value (minutes 5 to 10)

| Ask | Listen for | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| "What does three days get Godavari that nine does not?" | more loans sanctioned per month; capital disbursed sooner; fewer customers walking to the competitor; fewer audit questions | the value behind the number (Day 9 starts here) |
| "Which of those matters most to the board?" | one of them | the number to measure first |

## Who else (minutes 10 to 15)

| Ask | Listen for | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| "Who else has a stake in this: who benefits, who pays in time or budget?" | the team leads; "IT"; the branches | the rows he knows about |
| "Who has said no to something like this before, and why?" | the CISO, "security", "compliance had concerns in 2019" | the blockers by name; the 2019 pilot |
| "Who ran the last attempt, and where are they now?" | a name; sometimes the sceptic | the sceptic's row |

## Who can stop it (minutes 15 to 20)

| Ask | Listen for | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| "Who could stop this, inside the company?" | the CISO; the board's audit committee; "nobody, I have approved it" (which is the wrong answer and a finding) | the veto list |
| "And outside? What happens if the RBI asks about it at the next inspection?" | a pause; then the compliance head's name | the ghost and the proxy; whether the sponsor has thought about the auditor |
| "Is there anything you have promised someone that I should know about?" | a date to the board; a headcount plan that depends on this | the sponsor's fear; Day 30's bad-news conversation, prepared early |

## Who judges correctness (minutes 20 to 25)

| Ask | Listen for | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| "Who would you trust to say whether a file was processed correctly?" | a senior analyst; the compliance head; "the system will tell us" (wrong) | the eval owners |
| "If they say a file is wrong and the number says we are faster, who wins?" | the honest answer is "they do"; listen for whether he believes it | the first collision on the record (speed against error rate) |

Then say it in these words, so it is on the record: "You decide how fast it has to be. The people you just named decide what counts as right. We will measure both and show you both every week."

## The calendar (minutes 25 to 30)

| Ask | Listen for | Fills |
|---|---|---|
| "What is fixed in your calendar for the next six months?" | the RBI inspection; year end; the core-system freeze; board dates; festivals | the customer's calendar row |
| "When would a result be worth the most to you?" | before the board date; before the inspection | Day 8's sequencing |

## Before leaving

Ask for introductions, in his words, to every person he named: "Would you tell them you asked me to meet them?" An email from the COO opens the compliance head's door; a vendor's meeting request does not.

## Do not ask

- To describe the workflow in detail or estimate how long steps take (he will give you the SOP; the analysts and the tracker have the real numbers)
- To name the champion (a champion is found on the floor; one appointed from above is a liaison)
- Whether he wants a chatbot (he will say yes; Day 10 owns "not now, because")

## Within the hour

Fill the sponsor's row with the board sentence verbatim and the date. Add every name as a row. Put the calendar dates on the map. Rerun the script: if the sponsor's version and an operator's version are word for word the same, you have interviewed only one of them.
