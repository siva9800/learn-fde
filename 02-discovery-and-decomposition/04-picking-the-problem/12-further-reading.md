| Resource | Why read it |
|---|---|
| Atlassian, *Forward Deployed Engineering Playbook* (summary page; the PDF has the full playbook): https://www.atlassian.com/blog/guides-research/forward-deployed-engineering-playbook | "Pick the right problem: focus on issues that are frequent, painful, and measurable, then work backward to figure out what AI needs to solve them"; starting from a business outcome and mapping the work, people, systems, handoffs and permissions; "Curiosity drives initial adoption. | OpenAI forward deployed engineering, the 2025 interview summarised in the ZenML LLMOps database: https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/forward-deployed-engineering-bringing-enterprise-llm-applications-to-production | problems "representing tens of millions to low billions in value"; "genuinely high-stakes use cases at the core of the business rather than edge cases"; experts labelling evaluation sets before development; declining lucrative services work; the Morgan Stanley four months. Fetched October 2026 |
| OpenAI, Ramp, Nominal and Dataland, the 2026 panel summarised on the same site: https://www.zenml.io/llmops-database/forward-deployed-engineering-for-enterprise-llm-adoption | "carefully qualifying whether problems are genuinely FDE-shaped and have realistic paths to recurring revenue"; roadmap features over custom builds; recurring value over one-time consulting; when exploration is the right mode. Fetched October 2026 |
