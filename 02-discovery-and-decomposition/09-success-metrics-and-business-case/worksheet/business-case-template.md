# Baseline pack and business case · template

> Copy, rename, fill in order. Every number gets its method next to it. No model names on the case page.

## Part 1 · Baseline pack

1. **The report, read first.** For each reported figure: what it counts, what it leaves out, the caveat.
2. **The clock.** Whose clock, from which event to which event, in one sentence.
3. **The frame.** The source that cannot forget a unit; the window; why the window is ordinary (calendar check).
4. **Strata.** The groups that differ, and why; the allocation rule; the floor; the seed.
5. **The form.** One row per unit; every column names its source; the second-counter share.
6. **Results.** Each metric: report, hand count, 95 percent interval, consistent or not.
7. **What the report could not see.** The stage split, the addressable share, the risk rate, anything new.
8. **What the pack is not.**

## Part 2 · One-page case

| Block | Write |
|---|---|
| the decision asked for | one sentence |
| today, measured | the baseline with intervals and a one-line method |
| what changes | the mechanism as arrows from system to operational to business metric |
| the money | benefits (capacity as hires not made; retention; others), costs (licence, cloud in the customer's account, the customer's staff time, the learning dip), net, one-time |
| the floor and the break-even | the case on measured levers only; the value the unmeasured lever must reach, in words the sponsor can judge |
| not counted | each line with its value and why it is not added |
| what breaks it | one-way sensitivity on every assumption; a two-way on the two that matter most |
| promised, as ranges | per stakeholder: range, date, population, baseline |
| what decides it | the eval threshold, the pilot measure, the open commercial questions, the pre-registered definitions |

## Part 3 · Definitions to pre-register

For each metric: name · definition · unit and statistic · population · exclusions · source · baseline ·
target · "a change is claimed when" · measured by and when · guardrail · signatures.

## Checks before it leaves your desk

- [ ] the report's method read and its caveats written
- [ ] frame from the source that cannot forget a unit; seed recorded; no re-draw
- [ ] every headline number has an interval and a method
- [ ] loaded rate built line by line, allocations excluded, productive hours used
- [ ] capacity and cash kept apart; no hour counted twice
- [ ] one-time gains not annualised
- [ ] the customer's own staff time and the learning dip in the costs
- [ ] both ledgers (vendor and customer) reconciled line by line
- [ ] the floor and the break-even stated
- [ ] the eval threshold the case needs is named
- [ ] definitions signed by the sponsor and the compliance (or risk) owner before the pilot
