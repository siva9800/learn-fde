Credit Operations Circular No. GFL/CO/2025/027
Subject: Income proof for two-wheeler loans by applicant type
Date: 18 March 2025 · Effective: 01 April 2025
Supersedes: GFL/CO/2024/015
1. Purpose
1.1 This circular sets out the income proof accepted for two-wheeler loans, by the type of
applicant for all branches and for credit operations in Hyderabad and Vijayawada.
2. Scope
2.1 The rules apply to every two-wheeler application received on or after the effective
date, including files returned to a branch and resubmitted.
2.2 Where an applicant fits two types, the analyst applies the row for the main source of
income and records the reason in the file.
2.3 The requested amount and the finance percentage are checked separately under the
two-wheeler product rulebook; this circular covers only income proof.

3. General conditions
3.1 Every income document must be legible, must name the applicant, and must be in the file
of record. A photograph received on a personal phone is not part of the file of record.
3.2 A bank statement must be the full statement for the period, every page, as issued by the
bank or downloaded from the bank's portal; a passbook is accepted only if every page for the
period is present.
3.3 Self-declarations are on the Godavari format, signed by the applicant, and countersigned
by the branch manager where this circular says so.
4. Income proof by applicant type
4.1 The table below lists the income proof accepted for each type of applicant. Any one
complete option in the second column is enough.
4.2 Table 1: Income proof by applicant type
| Applicant type | Acceptable income proof | Conditions |
| Salaried | last three salary slips and the bank statement of the salary account for three months | statement ends within 30 days before the application |
