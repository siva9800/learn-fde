# Part 0 · Start Here: The Engineer in the Room

> What a forward deployed engineer is, told as one story, plus the three buckets, the promise and the map of the whole course · Day 0 · 1 episode · ~9 min

*Why this Part exists in 2026: "forward deployed engineer" is the fastest-growing engineering title in AI, and most people who search for it cannot say in one sentence what the job is. Vendors have models and platforms; customers have messy data, old systems, regulators and people who must change how they work on a Tuesday. The FDE is the engineer sent across that gap. Day 0 tells that story once, so that every later chapter can simply move the engagement forward. It also sets the two modes of using AI that this course shares with learn-ai-ml, because an FDE who lets the agent think for them will not survive the customer's first hard question.*

---

### Day 0 · The engineer in the room: what an FDE is, and the course map (~9 min)
- **Status:** new.
- **What you learn:** what an FDE does, in one story: an engineer sent into a lender that bought a document model turns it, over twelve weeks, into a system forty credit-ops analysts use every day, in the lender's environment and under its rules. From the story: the three buckets, the promise, the course map, the two modes of using AI.
- **Topics:**
  - The story: a vendor's model, a lender's loan-operations floor, the gap between them
  - The definition: the engineer a vendor sends in to make its product work inside someone else's company
  - The three buckets (core engineering, applied AI, discovery) and how the 2026 market weights them
  - The promise: one engagement end to end, then a second customer to prove transfer
  - The course map: 3 Stages, 9 Parts, Days 0 to 46, who can start where
  - The running engagement: Kaagaz AI, Godavari Finance, the COO, 40 analysts, the CISO
  - Learning mode and building mode: AI as tutor, AI as pair, the engineer owns the result
- **Real-time example:** the COO wants loan turnaround from 9 days to 3; the model can read a salary slip. Nobody has asked which of the 14 documents the analysts wait on, whether the 2009 core system can accept the answer, or whether the CISO lets a PDF leave the building. The FDE's first week goes on those questions. That is the engagement you run from Day 4.
- **Analogy (Telugu audience):** the specialist sits in a cold room and the patient must come. The family doctor comes to the house, sees the kitchen, the water and the grandmother who refuses tablets, and makes the treatment work there. The FDE is the family doctor of software.
- **Shorts:**
  - "The fastest-growing engineering title in AI, explained with one loan file."
  - "AI as tutor or AI as ghostwriter: the habit that decides whether you learn this."
- **Callbacks:** sets up Day 1 (origins), Day 2 (the buckets and the 60/30/10 week), Day 4 (the engagement begins), Day 42 (the second customer). The two modes are owned by learn-ai-ml Day 0; call back, never re-teach.
